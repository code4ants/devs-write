---
title: The Catalogue Room
---
The observatory went quiet after midnight. Ilinca liked it that way: the dome sealed, the telescopes asleep under their canvas, and only the catalogue room still lit, a long vault of drawers that smelled of dust and lavender polish. She had been filing glass plates for three hours when she found the envelope.

It was tucked between two plates from the winter of 1907, addressed in her own handwriting. The postmark was dated next Thursday.

~~~map {map="map0"}
1210, 905
~~~

She set it on the reading desk and did not open it. Outside, the sky was still black, but the stars over the eastern ridge had started to thin. *Not yet*, she thought. *Not before someone else has seen it.*

> *Whatever is inside was written by a version of me who already knows how this ends.*
{.callout}

-- Tomi, are you still up in the dome?

-- Always. The heater is on strike again. What did you find?

-- A letter. From me.

-- That is either very romantic or very bad.

She typed the plate number into the relay console. The machine took its time, as it always did, humming to itself like someone remembering a song.

~~~terminal {title="relay console // catalogue"}
$ lookup --plate 1907-B
plate 1907-B  northern field, exp. 40 min
filed:        1907-12-02
annotations:  1
  > "see letter, drawer 14" (added 2026-11-12)
~~~

> Note from the archive rules: no object may leave the catalogue room before it is logged, photographed and signed for by two staff members.
{.callout .blue}

The annotation was dated next week too.

---

Professor Varga arrived at four, as he always did, carrying two cups of tea and a newspaper he never read. He listened to the whole story without interrupting, which worried her more than any question would have.

~~~chat {title="Night shift"}
Ilinca | left | Professor, the plate annotation is dated the twelfth.
Varga | right | Then we have six days to deserve it.
Varga | right | Do not open the letter.
Tomi | left | I'm opening the dome. If something is coming, I want to see it first.
Ilinca | left | Nothing is coming. It is a filing error.
Varga | right | It is your handwriting, Ilinca.
~~~

> The plate shows a star that is not in any other catalogue. Tomi is already pointing the big refractor at the spot.
{.callout .green}

> Do not let anyone read the letter aloud. Whatever it says becomes true the moment it is spoken.
{.callout .red}

> Every archive is a letter to the future. Most of them simply take longer to arrive.

The last quote was painted above the door of the catalogue room, and she had walked under it every night for four years without reading it once.
