---
title: Him
---


Today is my 38th birthday. It's 8:30 p.m., and I'm halfway through my third job of the day.  

I’m an engineer, or at least I used to be one. Things aren’t the way they used to be. After the AI era began, only the best engineers managed to stay on top. The rest of us were left with low salaries and mostly manual work. 

My second job is stocking shelves. It only takes a few hours and the work is easy enough. My third job is cleaning office buildings. It's hard work, but it pays well.  

Today I’m at the XY company, a building filled with rental offices. This is where the highly skilled engineers work, the ones that survived the new era, the ones that earn more money that most people could ever dream of.

I could have been one of them. But then my health problems began, so I decided to take medical leave. After that, the world became an inferno.

I left my home town for a bigger city. where finding work is easier. This is also one of my favorite places. My grandparents lived here when I was little and I have quite some memories of this city. A dear old friend lived here once too. 

Now I rent a cozy one-room apartment. I've made it a home, even though I barely have time to enjoy it. I need to work enough to cover the medical expenses, that’s why I hardly ever spend time in my own little sanctuary.

Anyway, today is my birthday, and I have a plan: a bottle of wine all to myself. 

I don't have any friends here. I haven't managed to make any with all this work going on, and friendship isn’t what it used to be. These days, people work just to survive. There’s little time left for fun, especially when everything is so expensive. 

The world has split into two categories, super rich and super poor. 

I’ve been saving up money for the last four months so I could afford this bottle of wine. Four months. Just for one bottle. And I can barely contain my excitement at the thought of finally tasting it.

I will finish work quickly, go home, pour myself a glass, and spend the evening with a book. Maybe, for once, I'll get to enjoy my own company without worrying about tomorrow.

Tonight, I only have one small office to clean, since my colleagues take care of the rest. 

I approach the room. Something feels different today, though.

The light is on. Not the usual harsh, white light, either. Actually it is diffuse and warm, spilling into the hallway. As I get closer and closer I hear soft music playing inside.

I hear something else.

Whispers? 
Heavy breathing?
Moans? 

What the heck is going on in there?!

I step into the room.

A mature man, around fifty, is kissing a young woman. They're pressed against the wall, completely absorbed in each other. He's wearing a wedding ring. She isn't.

He's pulled her toward him, his body between her legs, her skirt pushed up. Her blouse hangs open, and her hands are all over him.

I should look away. I know I should.

But something holds my attention.

His build. There's something about the way he stands, the shape of his shoulders. Something terribly familiar.

I don't know anyone here. I shouldn't recognize him.
And yet...

I'm so lost in thought that I forget to hide.

The woman notices me first.

A split second later, he turns.

Our eyes meet.

I freeze.

It's him.

The love of my life.

No. No, no, no.

I need to leave.

Move.

Why can't I move?

Where are my thoughts? Why isn't my brain working?

Go. Just go!

After what feels like an eternity, my body finally obeys. I mumble an apology and rush out of the room, heading straight for the bathrooms.

My heart is pounding so hard I can barely breathe.

I have to keep moving. I have to clean something. Anything.

Because if I stop, I'll have to face what I just saw.
