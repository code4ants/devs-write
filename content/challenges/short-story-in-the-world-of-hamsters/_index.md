title: Short story in the world of hamsters
---
Write a short story set in a world where hamsters are the ones in charge: they have cities, rules, rivalries and legends. Bring us a day in the life of a wheel-runner, a burrow engineer, a seed merchant or a very suspicious cat-watcher. Use as many or as few of the available blocks (callouts, chat, terminal, scene breaks) as you like.
